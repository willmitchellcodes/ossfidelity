Open weights aren't the whole story

The Open Source AI Definition asks for three things: the weights, the code, and enough about the training data to rebuild it. Most "open" models share only the first.

Draft. These openness ratings come from model cards and our research notes. Before the workshop, each one is being checked against the European Open Source AI Index. The task benchmark runs for the first time once the site is on GitHub.

A small test anyone can rerun
