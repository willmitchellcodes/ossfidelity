How this will be scored

Each question gets 2, 1, or 0 points, all weighted equally, because there's no evidence that one matters more for everyone. A service gets a score only once all seven answers are verified. Red flags cap the score at 40 no matter how the rest look:

  • Chats can become public by default, or with one tap
  • Human-reviewed chats are kept more than a year after you delete them
  • There's no self-serve way to opt out of training

This covers consumer accounts only. A school or work account (for example, Microsoft 365 Copilot through your university) may have very different terms, so check with your IT office.
